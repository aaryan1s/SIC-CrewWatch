CrewWatch

AI-Powered Workforce Safety Monitoring

CrewWatch is an AI-powered workforce safety monitoring platform that uses computer vision to detect PPE compliance in real time across images, videos, webcams, and CCTV/RTSP streams. It helps improve workplace safety through intelligent monitoring, automated inspection reports, and live compliance analytics.
